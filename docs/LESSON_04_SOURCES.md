## Pedagogical safeguards

1. The mnemonic سَأَلْتُمُونِيهَا is not a mechanical root-extraction algorithm.
2. A letter in the mnemonic may be an original root consonant, as in سَأَلَ and وَجَدَ.
3. A correct analysis needs the word's pattern and lexical family, and should be checked against a reliable dictionary or teacher.
4. This lesson introduces triliteral verb roots only. Quadriliteral roots and weak-root changes are studied later.
5. Mujarrad/mazīd labels here refer to verb derivation. Do not confuse derivational additions with ordinary conjugation prefixes and suffixes.
6. The Form III and Form X glosses are lexical meanings, not universal meanings that can be mechanically assigned to every verb in those forms.
7. No textbook page references are assigned to Mīzān uṣ-Ṣarf, Mīzān wa Munshaʿib, or Dānesh-e Ṣarf until exact editions are identified and inspected.
