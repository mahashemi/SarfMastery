## Pedagogical safeguards

1. The 14-slot Sarf table is a teaching arrangement. Do not tell learners there are 14 distinct independent pronoun spellings; the second-person dual pronoun is the same for both genders.
2. The third-person masculine dual and feminine dual verb forms are different: كَتَبَا vs. كَتَبَتَا.
3. The second-person masculine and feminine dual slots share كَتَبْتُمَا; keep both slots in the traditional order but explain the shared form.
4. Distinguish masculine plural كَتَبُوا from feminine plural كَتَبْنَ.
5. Distinguish second-person masculine singular كَتَبْتَ from feminine singular كَتَبْتِ.
6. This table uses a sound Form I verb. Weak, hamzated, doubled, and augmented verbs require additional rules and are treated later.
7. The English gloss “you” does not encode gender or dual number; keep the Arabic pronoun and role label visible.
8. No textbook page references are assigned to Mīzān uṣ-Ṣarf, Mīzān wa Munshaʿib, or Dānesh-e Ṣarf until exact editions are identified and inspected.
