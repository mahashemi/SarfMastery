## Pedagogical safeguards

1. The 14-slot table is a traditional Sarf learning arrangement. It does not imply 14 distinct surface forms; some slots share the same conjugated form.
2. The second-person dual is the same for masculine and feminine. Third-person feminine dual and second-person dual also share تَكْتُبَانِ.
3. تَكْتُبُ can mean “she writes” (هِيَ) or “you write” (أَنْتَ); context or an explicit pronoun disambiguates it.
4. The four prefix letters are أ، ن، ي، ت. Do not confuse the prefix with the root letters.
5. The lesson shows the default indicative (marfūʿ) forms. Nasb and jazm after particles such as لَنْ and لَمْ are taught later.
6. The muḍāriʿ may express present, ongoing, or future action depending on context and particles; do not map it mechanically to one English tense.
7. The table uses the sound Form I verb كَتَبَ. Weak, hamzated, doubled, and augmented verbs require further rules.
8. No textbook page references are assigned to Mīzān uṣ-Ṣarf, Mīzān wa Munshaʿib, or Dānesh-e Ṣarf until exact editions are identified and inspected.
