## Claim-to-source map

| Claim | Supporting source | Scope / limitation |

| Ism zamān denotes the time of an action; ism makān denotes its place. | *Arabic: An Essential Grammar*, sections on nouns of time and place; *Basic Arabic: A Grammar and Workbook*, derived nouns section. | Context may determine whether a form is interpreted as time or place; some forms are lexicalised. |

## Pedagogical safeguards
1. Do not imply that مَفْعَلٌ and مَفْعِلٌ can be selected mechanically without knowing the verb class and attested word.
2. Do not conflate time/place nouns with the maṣdar or the active/passive participles.
3. Some lexical items have more than one possible semantic reading depending on context.
4. Do not claim that all instrument nouns are derived by one of the three patterns; many are lexicalised.
5. No textbook page references are assigned until the exact Hawza textbook editions are identified and inspected.
