## Pedagogical safeguards

1. The passive muḍāriʿ pattern for a sound Form I verb is يُفْعَلُ. Do not generalise this exact internal pattern to every derived form or weak verb.
2. The four prefix letters remain أ، ن، ي، ت, but the passive Form I prefix is vowelled with ḍammah in this model.
3. The table shows default indicative (marfūʿ) forms. Naṣb and jazm are deferred.
4. The 14-slot arrangement retains masculine/feminine dual slots; some surface forms are shared, including تُنْصَرُ and تُنْصَرَانِ.
5. The muḍāriʿ may refer to present or future action depending on context and particles.
6. Passive voice does not express the doer; the affected participant is represented as nāʾib al-fāʿil in a full sentence.
7. No textbook page references are assigned to Mīzān uṣ-Ṣarf, Mīzān wa Munshaʿib, or Dānesh-e Ṣarf until exact editions are identified and inspected.
