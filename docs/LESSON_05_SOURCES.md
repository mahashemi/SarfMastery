## Pedagogical safeguards

1. فَعَلَ is the basic model for a triliteral root, not the weight of every triliteral word.
2. The scale must match both the letters and the vowel/sukūn pattern of the word.
3. The first, second, and third root consonants correspond to ف، ع، ل. Do not replace added letters with these model letters.
4. Added letters are retained at their positions in the scale. Some patterns also involve doubling (shadda), deletion, or sound changes; these are deferred to later lessons.
5. A quadriliteral root commonly uses فَعْلَلَ as its basic scale. This lesson does not yet teach quadriliteral derivation.
6. A weight describes structure, not a guaranteed lexical meaning. The same pattern may have different meanings across verbs.
7. No page references are assigned to Mīzān uṣ-Ṣarf, Mīzān wa Munshaʿib, or Dānesh-e Ṣarf until exact editions are identified and inspected.
