## Pedagogical safeguards

1. الْمَاضِي الْمَجْهُولُ is the grammatical passive past; “majhūl” does not mean that the statement is uncertain.
2. The passive past pattern for a sound Form I triliteral verb is فُعِلَ. Do not generalise this exact internal pattern to every derived form or weak verb.
3. The person/number suffixes follow the same 14-slot teaching order as the active past; the internal vowel pattern changes.
4. The table uses نَصَرَ → نُصِرَ to make the passive meaning natural in all person/number forms.
5. The Qur’anic example كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الصِّيَامُ is analysed morphologically only. Do not present the morphology note as tafsīr.
6. The 14-slot arrangement retains the masculine/feminine dual slots; the two second-person dual slots share the same surface form.
7. No textbook page references are assigned to Mīzān uṣ-Ṣarf, Mīzān wa Munshaʿib, or Dānesh-e Ṣarf until exact editions are identified and inspected.
