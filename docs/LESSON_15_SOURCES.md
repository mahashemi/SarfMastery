## Pedagogical safeguards
1. Do not imply that the Form I فَاعِلٌ / مَفْعُولٌ patterns cover all root classes without modification.
2. Explain that participles are nouns/adjectival forms, not finite verbs marked for person and tense.
3. The passive participle normally presupposes a transitive/passivisable sense; do not mechanically generate one for every intransitive verb.
4. The derived-form pattern explanation is a preview. Later lessons must separately teach weak, hamzated, and doubled roots and their changes.
5. Preserve full tashkīl and distinguish the maṣdar (action), active participle (agent), and passive participle (patient).
6. No page references are assigned to the selected Hawza textbooks until their exact editions are verified.
