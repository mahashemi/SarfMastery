## Pedagogical safeguards
1. Distinguish original hamza in the root from hamzat al-waṣl used to ease pronunciation or introduced by a pattern.
2. Teach the position of hamza by identifying the three root radicals, not by looking only at the written word.
3. Expand the shadda in the doubled examples into two identical radicals when writing the root.
4. Do not imply every shadda in Arabic is evidence of a doubled root; shadda has other grammatical and phonological functions.
5. Full hamza spelling rules, weak-root transformations, and idghām are outside the scope of this introduction.
6. No textbook page references are assigned until the exact Hawza textbook editions are identified and inspected.
