## Pedagogical safeguards
1. Do not present the overview as a complete list of iʿlāl rules. The conditions are detailed and paradigm-dependent.
2. Teach root analysis alongside the visible form. The alif in قَالَ reflects the weak middle radical of ق و ل.
3. The disappearance of initial wāw in وَعَدَ → يَعِدُ is a common pattern, not a universal rule for every initial-wāw root.
4. Distinguish the weak-letter deletion in لَمْ يَدْعُ from other changes to final-weak verbs.
5. Distinguish doubled-root idghām (مَدَّ) from the separated radicals when a suffix intervenes (مَدَدْتُ).
6. A shadda can arise for reasons other than a doubled lexical root; interpret it in context.
7. No page references are assigned until the exact Hawza textbook editions are identified and inspected.
