# Product Specification

## Identity and audience
- Product: **Sarf Mastery — صَرْف**.
- No personal-name branding or personal signature anywhere in the app.
- Primary audience: absolute beginners and Hawza students.
- Primary language: English; Persian and Urdu support planned.
- Daily commitment: 20–30 minutes.

## Learning outcomes
1. Understand traditional Sarf terminology and sequence.
2. Read and reproduce common conjugation tables.
3. Identify roots, patterns, forms, and derived nouns.
4. Analyse unfamiliar words with appropriate caution.
5. Use Sarf while reading Qur'an and hadith without confusing morphology with tafsir.
6. Explain core concepts to another beginner.

## Pedagogy
Learn → Understand → Memorise → Apply → Explain → Teach.

Never assume the learner knows Arabic grammatical terminology, root-and-pattern analysis, short vowels, pronoun categories, dual forms, or conjugation tables.

Every major concept should answer: What is it? Why do we need it? How does it work? What are several examples? What mistakes do beginners make? How is it different from similar concepts? How do I recognise it in a new word? Can I teach it?

## Lesson structure
Objective; fully vowelled Arabic term; transliteration; plain-English definition; Persian/Urdu support; reason the concept matters; stepwise explanation; multiple examples; common questions; guided and independent practice; verified Qur'an/Hadith application where appropriate; audio or explicit unavailable state; recall; teach-back; source notes.

## Responsive UX
- Desktop: sidebar, spacious reading column, large conjugation tables, paired explanation/practice.
- Tablet: collapsible navigation, adaptive columns, touch-friendly controls.
- Mobile: single-column lessons, compact navigation, prominent Arabic and audio controls, no unintended horizontal overflow.
- Arabic RTL, English LTR, Persian/Urdu RTL. Correct mixed-direction punctuation and numerals.
- Use Arabic typography with reliable tashkīl positioning.

## Audio
Prefer human-verified Arabic recordings, normal and slow playback, listen-and-repeat. Browser TTS is only a labelled fallback. Never show functional-looking audio controls without a real source.

## Progress and privacy
First version works without an account. Keep progress on-device. Add export/import before considering cloud sync. Explain local data storage.

## Integrity
Verify Qur'an and hadith references and attribute narrations carefully. Never invent textbook pages, quotations, or audio provenance. Keep morphology distinct from tafsir. Write original explanations and exercises; do not reproduce protected textbook chapters.
